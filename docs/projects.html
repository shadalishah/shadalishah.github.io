Pakistan Macroeconomic Dynamics

Focus: Macroeconomics, Econometrics, and Time-Series Analysis Tools: VAR, SVAR, VECM, ARIMA, R, Stata, and Quarto

  • Analysed Pakistan’s 2010–2020 quarterly GDP, spending, revenue, inflation, and interest-rate data to assess fiscal-monetary linkages.
  • Applied ADF/KPSS, ARIMA/ARMA, VAR, Granger causality, IRFs, FEVD, and short-run identified SVAR in Python.
  • Identified mixed I(0)/I(1) series and no cointegration, supporting first-differenced VAR; delivered a reproducible policy-analysis report.

Pakistan E-Commerce Analytics

Focus: Customer Analytics, Segmentation, and Forecasting Tools: Python, Pandas, K-Means, Prophet, and Statistical Analysis

  • Cleaned 1,048,575 raw rows into 584,524 records with 32 engineered features and analysed 115,326 customers.
  • Applied RFM/K-Means segmentation, Prophet/LightGBM forecasting, and XGBoost-SHAP churn modelling to identify customer and sales patterns.

Advanced SQL Analytics with Google BigQuery

Focus: SQL Analytics, Customer Analysis, and KPI Reporting Tools: SQL, Google BigQuery, CTEs, and Window Functions

  • Analysed 50,000 e-commerce customer records to assess data quality, churn, retention, and executive KPIs using Google BigQuery and SQL; identified a 28.9% overall churn rate across 14,450 records.
  • Compared churned and retained customers using SQL aggregations, CTEs, and behavioural analysis; found lower engagement among churned records, including 9.12 vs 12.64 average logins and 64.18% vs 54.19% cart abandonment.
  • Segmented customers by lifetime value and behavioural risk using NTILE(), window functions, CTEs, and joins; identified a High Risk segment with 90.28% observed churn and quantified $7.23M lifetime value at risk in the US.

Pakistan and Global Employment Analytics

Focus: Labour Economics, Policy Analysis, and Business Intelligence Tools: Tableau, World Bank Data, Trend Analysis, and Forecasting

  • Analysed ~57,500 employment-rate observations across 183 countries from 1991–2025 to assess Pakistan’s employment trends by gender and benchmark global performance.
  • Built an interactive Tableau dashboard using trend analysis, forecasting, data visualization, and country/sex filters to project Pakistan’s employment trends through 2030.
  • Found male employment declined from 90.61% (1998) to 83.54% (2020), while female employment more than doubled from 13.39% (1995) to 25%+ by 2025; female employment was forecast at 25.53% for 2030.

NewsSense-BERT

Focus: Natural Language Processing, Text Analytics, and Transformer Research Tools: Python, Pandas, NLTK, Scikit-learn, Named Entity Recognition, and BERT

BankWise AI RAG Assistant

Focus: Generative AI, Banking Information, and Retrieval-Augmented Generation Tools: Python, NLP, and RAG

  • Built a banking support assistant using a 1,000-entry knowledge base to provide grounded answers to domain-specific banking questions and reduce hallucination risk.
  • Designed an end-to-end RAG pipeline using LangChain, FAISS, Hugging Face embeddings, and Google Gemini, with 500-character chunking and 50-character overlap for context-preserving retrieval.
